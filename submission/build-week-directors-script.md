# Build Week — Evidence Briefing Desk Recording Script

**Segment length:** 45–60 seconds  
**Use in:** the product-proof portion of the Build Week demo video  
**Start:** `http://localhost:3000/evidence-desk`, then click **Reset demo to
corroborated case**.

## Goal

Show the operator decision moment: evidence can corroborate a sighting,
contradict it, or be too incomplete to act on. RAVENCRY makes that distinction
clear while keeping alert publication under human control.

| Time | On-screen direction | Audio / voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:05 | Start on **Fatima Bello**. Keep the header and three evidence cards visible. | “RAVENCRY brings fragmented reports into one source-linked briefing for a human responder.” |
| 0:05–0:16 | Move down the three cards. Pause on Fatima Bello and Sabon Gari Market, Kano. | “A citizen report, a Voice Relay sighting, and a community radio signal independently converge on Fatima and Sabon Gari Market.” |
| 0:16–0:25 | Show **Ready for human review**, **Corroborations**, and enabled **Approve for existing alert workflow**. | “The Desk separates supported facts from open questions, making the safe operator decision obvious: this case is ready for human review.” |
| 0:25–0:31 | Click approval. Pause on **Review recorded — escalation prepared** and **No alert was sent by this desk.** | “Approval records a human review step. It does not send an alert; the approved workflow remains separate.” |
| 0:31–0:43 | Click **Conflicting locations**. Show Kaduna Central Mosque, Kawo Bus Stop, the conflict state, and disabled approval. | “When sources conflict, RAVENCRY does not widen outreach. It exposes both locations, asks for verification, and blocks approval.” |
| 0:43–0:50 | Click **Reset demo to corroborated case**. Pause on returned Fatima state. | “One click restores the known starting point, so an operator can review the evidence path again.” |
| 0:50–0:56 | Hold on the operator gate. | “This is not an autonomous alert engine. It is a traceable briefing desk that keeps the final decision with people.” |

## Recording checklist

- [ ] Fatima corroborated state opens the segment.
- [ ] The three sources, corroborations, conflict state, and disabled approval are legible.
- [ ] The **No alert was sent by this desk** safety line is visible after approval.
- [ ] Do not claim live API data, autonomous broadcast, or runtime model behavior unless implemented and shown elsewhere in the video.
- [ ] Record this segment at 1080p if practical and keep it under one minute.

## Transition

Follow this product segment with the Build Week implementation story: where
Codex accelerated the work, the key safety decisions, and the GPT-5.6 live
briefing upgrade only after that behavior is implemented and demonstrated.

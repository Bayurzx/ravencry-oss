From Report to Rescue

AI-powered triage ensures the right people get the right alert at the right time.

1

Report

Citizen reports via WhatsApp, Telegram, USSD, or web. Voice notes in Hausa auto-transcribed by Whisper AI.

2

AI Triage

Qwen LLM extracts name, location, circumstance. Classifies severity: FLASH (child), PRIORITY, or ROUTINE.

3

Verify

Anti-fake detection flags suspicious reports. Operator reviews and approves FLASH cases in under 60 seconds.

4

Broadcast

Geofenced alerts blast to Telegram, SMS, Web Push, and NAPTIP — all within 90 seconds of the original report.

Three-Tier Alert System

Inspired by the AMBER Alert model, adapted for Nigeria's unique security landscape.

⚡ FLASH

Immediate Broadcast

Child abductions, high-risk states (Zamfara, Borno, Kaduna), weapons involved. Broadcast within 90 seconds.

  • Child under 14 abducted in Zamfara
  • Multiple victims, armed kidnappers
  • Witness report with vehicle plate
🔶 PRIORITY

Urgent Investigation

Adult missing in dangerous area, ransom demands, or OSINT-corroborated case. Reviewed within 4 hours.

  • Adult missing >24h in conflict zone
  • OSINT news correlates with citizen report
  • Multiple reports for same incident
✅ ROUTINE

Monitored Case

Low-risk missing person, possibly voluntary. Listed on platform, periodic OSINT sweep. Reviewed within 24 hours.

  • Adult missing in urban area, no foul play
  • Elderly person not returned home
  • Runaway teenager (no danger indicators)
